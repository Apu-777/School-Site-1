Clubs & Athletics

32 student-run clubs and 14 varsity teams, from robotics and debate to soccer and crew. If you show up, you play — cuts only happen at the varsity level in a small number of sports.

The Commons

Our student center runs on a rotating menu voted on by a student food committee each term.

Outdoor Program

Every grade takes a fall expedition, from a two-day hike in 6th grade to a week-long backcountry trip by senior year.

Student Government

An elected student council sits on the school's facilities and dining committees with a real vote, not an advisory one.

Recognition

State Robotics Finalist

Ridgeview's FIRST Robotics team has reached state finals three years running, 2024–2026.

Regional Debate Champions

The debate team won the Northern Vermont Invitational in 2025, its first regional title.

Published Young Poets

Four students have had work accepted into the state's Young Poets anthology since 2022.
