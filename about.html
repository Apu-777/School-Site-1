What Ridgeview is, and isn't.

We're not interested in prestige for its own sake. We're interested in students who leave here knowing how to ask a good question, defend an idea, and fix something that's broken.

Mission

To teach every student to think independently, work with their hands, and take responsibility for a community — not just a transcript.

Vision

A school small enough that no student goes unnoticed, and rigorous enough that none goes unchallenged.

Our Values

Four things we won't compromise on.

Known, not sorted

No academic tracking before high school. Every student's path stays open until they choose it.

Made, not just studied

Every department includes hands-on, project-based work — not just lecture and exam.

Need-blind aid

Financial aid decisions are made separately from admissions, and can cover up to 100% of tuition.

Community, kept small

Enrollment is capped at 420 students by design, even when demand would allow more.
