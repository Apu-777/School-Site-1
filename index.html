Our Approach

Small enough to know your name. Ambitious enough to push you.

Taught, not tracked

No sorting students into tiers in 6th grade. Every path stays open until a student chooses it.

Studio time, weekly

A standing block every week for independent projects, research, or making something with your hands.

Faculty who stay

Average teacher tenure at Ridgeview is 11 years. You're taught by people who chose to stay.
